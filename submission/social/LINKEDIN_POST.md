Missing one lecture can turn into a week of stress. For the Decoding Data Science Agentic AI Challenge I built
**CatchUp AI**, a study-recovery copilot for students who fall behind.

What it does:
📄 Turns your own lecture PDFs into a catch-up pack: a summary, key terms and check questions, each linked to its page
🤖 A copilot that calls real tools (your deadlines, availability and notes) and cites the page for every fact
🧮 A deterministic planner that shows three recovery routes and tells you honestly when a plan doesn't fit
✅ Nothing is saved until you approve it, and approval commits exactly once
🌲 Progress you can see: finished study blocks grow a forest, and a recovery score shows if you're keeping up

How I tested it: 267 automated tests, a 38-case evaluation (8 cases are waiting on a live model and are reported as
skipped, not passed), a RAG evaluation with page-level ground truth, and an AI red-team pass.
Everything is public: https://catchupai-chi.vercel.app/quality

Try it (one-click demo, synthetic data): https://catchupai-chi.vercel.app
