# Volunteer test kit

A 20-minute session for one student volunteer, run by a facilitator. The goal is real evidence about
whether CatchUp AI helps a student who has fallen behind, collected honestly and without private data.

## Before the session

- **Site:** https://catchupai-chi.vercel.app
- **Account:** the volunteer clicks **Try the demo**. It creates a temporary account with synthetic data
  that is deleted after 24 hours. No sign-up or email is needed.
- **Data rules:** the volunteer must not upload real grades, health information or anyone else's work.
  Lecture notes they wrote themselves, or the synthetic notes in the demo, are fine.
- **Consent (read aloud):** "We're testing the app, not you. You can stop at any time. We'll write down
  what you do and say, without your name. Nothing you type is shared outside the app."
- **Facilitator:** open the notes template at the end of this page. Don't help unless the volunteer is
  stuck for more than a minute, and write down when you do.

## Tasks (read one at a time)

| # | Task for the volunteer | What to watch for |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | "You missed last week's statistics lecture. Find out what it covered." | Do they find **Catch-up pack**? Do they open a cited page? |
| 2 | "Check you understood it." | Do they answer the check questions? Do they re-read a page after a wrong answer? |
| 3 | "Ask the copilot what a residual is." | Do they notice the page citation and the separation between facts and suggestions? |
| 4 | "You have two deadlines and limited time. Make a plan you'd actually follow." | Do they compare the three routes? Do they understand why a route can't be approved? |
| 5 | "Before you approve it, decide whether you trust it." | Do they open **Why trust this?** What do they say about it? |
| 6 | "Mark one study block as done, then see how you're doing." | Do they find the tick box? Do they understand the recovery score? |
| 7 | "Tell the app what you thought." | Do they use 👍/👎 or **Preferences → Send feedback**? |

## Questions after the tasks

1. On a scale of 1–5, how likely would you be to use this the next time you fall behind? Why?
2. Which part, if any, saved you time?
3. Was anything confusing or untrustworthy?
4. Did you believe the plan would fit your week? What would make you believe it more?
5. What is the one thing you'd change?

## Reporting results

- Report only what happened: number of volunteers, tasks completed, time on task, direct quotes, and bugs.
  Never round up, combine sessions you didn't run, or describe planned sessions as done.
- In-app ratings appear as totals on the public [quality page](https://catchupai-chi.vercel.app/quality).
  Comments stay private to each account.
- File bugs as GitHub issues with the steps to reproduce. Don't include the volunteer's name.
